Disconnected Team Workflows

HR shift roster updates, IT ticket escalation, attendance records, and project billing information can become difficult to coordinate when they are managed in separate systems.

SAAIT Fix: Auto Team Queue Routing -

Ghost Attendance

Unverified Remote Clock-Ins

Remote and field-based employees can make attendance management more challenging when organizations rely on basic clock-in systems without additional verification.
Without appropriate controls, attendance records may be difficult to validate or audit.

SAAIT Fix: Geofenced Waiver Audit -

SAAIT Fix: Private Commentary RBAC Isolation
